# AIVOA.AI Discovery Challenge

Research-backed presentation prepared for the **AI Solutions Associate** discovery challenge.

The submission explores:

- The pharmaceutical quality-management problem AIVOA.AI appears to solve
- Likely customers and business value
- Biocon as a potential customer profile
- A possible multi-site quality-workflow challenge, clearly presented as a hypothesis
- Discovery questions that should be validated before recommending a solution

## Core takeaway

> AIVOA can reduce the manual work around pharmaceutical quality events while keeping regulated review, approval, and accountability with people.

## Research approach

I first reviewed AIVOA's public product and pricing pages to understand its target workflows and customer segments. I then reviewed Biocon and Biocon Biologics annual reports for primary evidence about quality systems and digital manufacturing initiatives.

The proposed Biocon challenge is an **evidence-based hypothesis**, not a claim of a confirmed quality or compliance failure. Product performance statements from AIVOA are treated as vendor claims unless independently validated.

## Presentation guidance

The deck is designed for a 3–5 minute camera-on, screen-shared explanation. Speaker notes contain prompts and source links. They are intended as preparation aids, not as a word-for-word script.
